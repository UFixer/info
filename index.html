COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

How does remote mobile repairing work?

We connect remotely to your computer using secure client software like TeamViewer or AnyDesk. You connect the mobile phone to your PC via a USB cable, and our expert technician performs the flashing, unlocking, or configuration steps remotely from our lab.

Is the remote repair process safe for my private data?

Yes, absolutely. The remote session is fully transparent; you can see everything our technician does on your screen in real time. We only access the diagnostic and flashing tools needed for the repair. Your personal data is completely secure.

What do I need to prepare before a remote repair session?

You need a stable internet connection, a Windows PC, and a high-quality USB data cable to connect your phone. Once you confirm the payment, we will guide you on installing the remote sharing software and phone drivers.

How do I pay and how do you confirm the payment?

You can pay via bank transfer (UBL Bank account or IBAN), mobile wallets (Raast/JazzCash), Skrill, or Crypto (USDT/USDC/SOL). After payment, simply share a screenshot of the transaction receipt or TXID on WhatsApp to start your remote session immediately.
